Control your Navien AirOne — ventilation, dehumidifier and air purifier — and Navien sleep mats from Homey, over the same Navien Smart cloud the official app uses: REST for control and AWS IoT MQTT for realtime state, so the app reflects what the appliance actually reports rather than an optimistic guess. This is an unofficial, community-built app and is not affiliated with Navien.

The AirOne gives you power, operating mode (auto, ventilate, ventilate + dehumidify, dehumidify, purify, cooking, sleep, bypass), fan speed, and a server-checked target humidity, alongside a full set of air-quality readings — PM1.0, PM2.5, PM10, CO₂, TVOC, radon, temperature, humidity, an overall air-quality score and filter usage — with "good / bad" grade labels for TVOC and radon. A paired AirMonitor is added as its own sensor device, and Navien sleep mats expose power, per-zone temperature and heat level. Everything is available to Homey Flow: any reading can be a condition, and mode, fan speed, power and target humidity can be actions. When a setting doesn't apply to the current mode — for example humidity in Auto — the app explains why instead of failing silently.

Setup is a single login: enter your Navien Smart account in the app settings, or during device pairing, and your appliances are found automatically. Only newer AirOne units (model code 1000 and above) are supported; older units keep a different protocol and cannot be reached this way.
